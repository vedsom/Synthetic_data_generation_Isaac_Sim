How this dataset was made

01 · SCENE

A USD warehouse in Isaac Sim with thousands of boxes, pallets, barrels, crates and bottles.

02 · LABELS

Every object carries a semantic class label on its USD prim. No manual drawing.

03 · CAMERAS

Virtual cameras placed along the aisles, like handheld scanners or CCTV.

04 · REPLICATOR

Renders RGB, segmentation, depth, 2D/3D boxes and occlusion for every frame, pixel-perfect.

05 · USE IT

Ready for training detectors and segmenters, or for testing perception before touching real hardware.
